My first repository
**Venom** is a 2018 American superhero film based on the **Marvel Comics** character of the same name.
It was produced by *Columbia Pictures* in association with *Marvel and Tencent Pictures* and distributed by *Sony Pictures Releasing*

# Main Characters
- **Eddie Brock / Venom** – portrayed by Tom Hardy
- **Anne Weying** – portrayed by Michelle Williams
- **Carlton Drake / Riot** – portrayed by Riz Ahmed

# Plot Summary
When journalist **Eddie Brock** tries to expose a corrupt scientist, he becomes the host of an **alien symbiote** named *Venom*.
Together, they form an unusual bond and become an anti-hero who must fight to survive against stronger enemies.

# Key Themes
**1. Duality of identity** – Eddie vs. Venom
**2. Power and control**
**3. Redemption and morality**

# Why I Like It
- The humor and chemistry between Eddie and Venom are **entertaining**.
- The **action scenes** are intense and visually stunning.
- Venom’s personality makes him a unique **anti-hero** compared to typical Marvel characters.
